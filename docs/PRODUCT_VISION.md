# Product Vision

## The problem

Communities with a low-resource heart language rarely have Bible video content
in their own language, with characters and settings they can see themselves in.
Producing that content by hand — casting culturally appropriate characters,
directing scenes, animating them, and matching speech to a real recording of the
language — is slow, expensive, and requires skills most missions/translation
teams don't have in-house.

## The vision

Given a chapter's existing heart-language audio narration and its translated
text, HeartWord autonomously produces a finished chapter video:

- breaks the chapter into scenes
- casts each character in the scene from a canon character library (indigenous
  in appearance, consistent across scenes and across chapters)
- directs the action for each scene
- generates the visuals for each scene
- lip-syncs each character's speech to the source-language audio
- assembles everything into a single, watchable chapter video

The output is a starting cut the user can watch end to end and then edit —
not a black box, and not meant to be the final untouched product.

## Example

Input: a narrator's recording of Mark 2 in the heart language, plus its
latinized/translated text.
Output: a chapter video of Mark 2, start to finish, with indigenous characters
speaking the heart language in lip-synced dialogue, ready to review and edit.

## Why this matters

Building tools with practical benefit to others — here, giving low-resource
language communities Bible content that looks and sounds like them — is the
point of this project.
