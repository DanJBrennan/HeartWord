# Mark 4:9 distinct listeners

Version 2 fixed every listener's eyeline toward Jesus, but the scene Critic
and duplicate detector found that clean-shaven John and the separate young
crowd witness shared a nearly identical face. This revision pins their two
different approved reference faces, hair visibility, nose/jaw shapes and
teal-versus-blue-grey garments. It keeps the good boat/shore composition,
bright morning light and closed mouths. Both previous candidates and their
review evidence remain retained.
