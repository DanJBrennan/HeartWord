# John continuity for the isolated Meyah Mark 4 edition

Dan approved a clean-shaven John son of Zebedee throughout this edition on
2026-10-26, then approved the exact standing reference in the isolated
comparison pack. This is an edition-specific visual decision. Historical
character versions, existing donor media, the live HeartWord database, and
Mark 5 Prep retain their own history.

The approved standing candidate is recorded with its source hashes and prompt
at `output/mark4-donor-20261024/assets/characters/john-son-of-zebedee/motion/motion-neutral-standing-v5-meyah-candidate.json`.
The v4 headshot, walking, teaching, and seated references were already
clean-shaven; their hashes are verified when the isolated v5 package is
promoted. The v4 standing reference had a short chin beard and must not be
used for newly generated Meyah footage.

The retained-picture audit samples the early, middle, and late frame of each
of 24 donor placements tagged with John. Recognizable John close-ups show a
clean-shaven face. In the wide and storm views, John is absent, obscured, or
too distant for facial-hair detail to justify replacing the approved picture.
Retain these placements while checking actual motion and story flow in the
assembled film. The audit is in
`output/mark4-donor-20261024/assets/review/meyah-mark4-john-retained-picture-20261026/`.

The private Mark 4:10–11 group keyframe candidate 3 predates this decision and
shows the old bearded reference. Its clean-shaven edit is only a visual
preview. A new formal candidate must use the approved v5 character package,
the current crew brief, and the ordinary keyframe Critic and producer review
before it can become a production start frame.
