# Mark 4:9 shore eyeline correction

The first candidate preserved five identities and the teaching boat, but
all four shore listeners gazed up and right while Jesus was on their left.
The scene Critic flagged this spatial contradiction at moderate severity.
This revision turns the whole listening group's heads and eyes toward Jesus,
while retaining the approved shot, canon faces and wardrobe, pebbled setting,
bright morning light and closed mouths. Candidate 1 and its QC stay in
history.
