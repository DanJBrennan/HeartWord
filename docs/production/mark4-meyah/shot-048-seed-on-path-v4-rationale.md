# Mark 4:14 broadcast start pose

The third candidate removed airborne seeds but left the palm static and cupped.
Its scene Critic rated this a moderate motion-feasibility concern and suggested
a drawn-back or outward-sweeping arm. This revision starts the lateral cast
with the forearm pulled back, hand angled toward the path and a small cluster
of seed still in contact with it. It also removes the accidental blue-trimmed
cuff that could identify the anonymous sower as Jesus. Prior candidates and
their QC remain in history.
