## Producer workflow

Select a chapter and use **Create / review storyboard**, or let the existing
autonomous casting stage invoke the same saved crew runner. Rufus supplies
the chapter progression and major beats with exact supplied Scripture triggers,
audience experience, character response, intensity and filmable means.
Each pause/cue explains purpose, timing basis, coverage and cost. Passage-specific
choices may include close-ups, anticipation, reactions, context views, openings,
endings, ambience, score entrances/releases and wordless space. No fixed intro
duration or pull-away ending is imposed.

Nell, Sam, Composer, Miriam and the three independent challengers assess the
treatment before keyframes. Both bounded planning passes include the critics;
30 role calls is the ceiling, not a target. A concern/uncertainty becomes an
existing blocking objection requiring explicit original-owner resolution.
The chapter's Production Crew panel displays the plan and verdict. Applicable
passing reviews and existing source/canon/cost/approval gates allow automatic
continuation; Dan does not sign each chapter plan.

Current accepted intent accompanies reconciled generation direction. Producer
edits retain precedence and history. Later briefs pin the treatment to its
planning run/hash. Nell uses the same existing picture/explicit-pause controls;
Sam/Composer resolve cues against the actual spaced cut. Speaking words, order,
speed and supplied on-camera performance stay fixed. New generation holds if
the treatment is absent/stale/held; old accepted media stays retained. A failed
new treatment run is explicitly labelled as requiring treatment, so it cannot
fall through to legacy finishing behavior after a missing/malformed reply or
interrupted run. Legacy review remains possible without invented plan evidence.

Finishing requires actual audiovisual evidence, Nell's timestamped verdict for
each accepted beat and sound/music verdicts on scene fit, transitions, levels,
speech priority, entrances/releases and mobile clarity. A concern or unavailable
evidence holds. A compressed proxy does not certify exact Hatam words, physical
phone playback, field approval or release. New music/ambience admission also
rejects unintelligible/gibberish voices, separately from intelligible dialogue.
