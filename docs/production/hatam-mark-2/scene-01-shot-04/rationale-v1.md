# Scene 1, Shot 4: production rationale

This is the first interior view of the roof opening in Mark 2:4. It inherits
the previously recorded continuity requirements: primary rafters must still
span the opening, its rim must be rough and debris-bearing, and the paralytic’s
`krabattos` is a flexible woven pallet rather than a framed bed. The lowering
has only begun; no healing is shown.

The key visual task is to communicate height, a packed room, and the suspended
mat while retaining clear placement for Jesus below. The friends are present at
the roof edge through hands/heads/upper bodies, keeping the action physically
legible without adding a second interior instance of them.

Acceptance: flat paralytic and level rope-supported woven mat; two visible
spanning rafters; irregular opening, reeds/earth/debris and light shafts;
packed crowd; Jesus and principal scribe each once; no healing, duplicate
characters, clean skylight-like roof, rigid bed or anachronistic materials.
