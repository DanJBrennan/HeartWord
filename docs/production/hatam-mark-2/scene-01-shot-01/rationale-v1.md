Hatam Mark 2 project `fe1408a5-d515-4d5b-b5c1-5fc55d0ae32d`.

The saved shot depicts Mark 2:1-2: an exterior crowd blocking the entrance.
Its camera is wide/static, crowd density packed, and roof still intact.
The eight cast records have approved Papuan v2 versions and clean single-pose
references, which were inspected before use. Their accepted garments remain
the authority; this is not another canon redesign.

The scene-level lighting string currently describes an interior and a later
roof opening. Applying that entire string to this exterior shot contradicts
its action and continuity. The prompt uses clear daylight outdoors and the
intact roof specified for this shot. Previous review found an inside-looking-out
viewpoint, fortress-like walls, and a posed crowd; the new composition addresses
those findings without changing the story or replanning the chapter.

The house uses modest basalt walls and a timber/reed/earth roof, supported by
the archaeological survey's domestic architecture discussion:
[Matthew J. Grey, Simon Peter in Capernaum](https://rsc.byu.edu/ministry-peter-chief-apostle/simon-peter-capernaum-archaeological-survey-first-century-village).
Exact composition is a production reconstruction, not a claim to know this
house's historical facade. No additional architecture research requirements
are imposed on the approved character garments.

Acceptance: outside-looking-in composition; house and intact roof legible;
blocked doorway and irregular dense crowd; approved cast identity/clothing;
photorealism and 16:9; no text, anachronisms, duplicate featured characters,
roof-opening action, Jesus or carriers introduced early. Save a new candidate,
visually review it, and retain the prior attempts for comparison.
