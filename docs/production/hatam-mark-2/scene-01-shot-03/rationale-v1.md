# Scene 1, Shot 3: production rationale

Mark 2:3-4 shows the four friends beginning to make an opening in the roof.
The database action’s word “tiles” conflicts with the documented Capernaum
construction standard. This prompt keeps the intended action and replaces only
that stale material with timber rafters, reeds/branches, and packed earth/clay.

The keyframe needs enough motion headroom for handheld-follow video generation:
work is underway, the mat remains on the roof, and lowering has not begun.
The paralytic lies flat on a flexible woven pallet and is not depicted as healed
or sitting up. Rafters remain because removing them would collapse the roof.

Acceptance: all four friends and paralytic appear once; early roof work with
intact spanning rafters; ragged partial opening and debris; historic materials,
costumes and 16:9 realism; no premature lowering or healing.
