# Proposed Control-Room Responsibilities

Status: proposed presentation and future-workflow reference, not production authority.

## Purpose

The control-room cast is a visual way for the producer to explore the film-production
roles named in the HeartWord product vision. It is not a runtime orchestration system.
Each profile may prepare, review, challenge, or explain a proposal, but cannot dispatch
production work, change Scripture, approve media, alter source audio, or publish a
render.

## Canonical proposal list

The complete, structured responsibility list is maintained in
[`src/server/control-room-responsibilities.ts`](../../src/server/control-room-responsibilities.ts).
That source supplies the presentation profile cards directly, so the visible bullets
cannot drift from this proposed role matrix.

Every profile has six responsibilities. They draw only from the existing HeartWord
product vision and design boundaries: verified Scripture remains controlling, the
producer retains every approval, canon and source-audio changes remain explicit, and
all presentation dialogue remains separate from production work.

## Scope

- Mabel, Rufus, Pippa, Gus, Vera, Orion, and the production crew represent proposed
  planning, generation-brief, review, and assembly responsibilities.
- Ada, Theo, and Cora are proposed challenge roles; they surface concerns but do not
  become automatic gates.
- Gene Siskel and Roger Ebert are presentation-only film-criticism roles; they have no
  production authority and do not imitate the real critics.
- The card content is read-only presentation context. It neither writes project records
  nor changes the existing Mark workflow.

## Review before adoption

Before any responsibility becomes a real production role, Dan should review the
wording, decide whether it belongs in an existing pipeline stage, and define what
evidence and producer approval would be required. Until then, every item remains a
proposed responsibility.
