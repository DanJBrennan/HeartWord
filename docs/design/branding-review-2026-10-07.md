# HeartWord naming decision

Research date: October 7, 2026. Status: recommendation ready for Dan; no new
name adopted. The established HeartWord branding remains authoritative.

Latest decision comparison: Dan has narrowed his choices to **Heart Language Media**
and **Word Into Light**. Between those two, recommend **Word Into Light**
for a shared organization/studio/output name, with the established tagline
“Scripture media for every heart language.” Heart Language Media is stronger
when direct mission description is the main priority. The detailed comparison
and refreshed domain evidence are recorded below. Dan has
not selected or adopted either name.

Dan subsequently requested 20 additional names containing Flix. That
domain-qualified exploration is recorded below; it does not establish Flix
as a final naming requirement or adopt another brand.

The initial review recommended **changing the public umbrella name to Word in Reach**. It keeps
the connection between Scripture and access, is easy to say, and supports
Studio, Films and Illustrated without restricting the organization to one
medium or one AI technology. HeartWord has a strong connection to heart
languages, but Dan's reported “heartworm” confusion, existing Bible products
using HeartWord, and an aftermarket .com weigh against retaining it.

## Product goal and acceptance

The product remains an autonomous AI production studio for source-faithful
Scripture media in heart languages. Verified Scripture, the source-audio
performance and approved regional canon lead to editable chapter films;
illustrated outputs remain a related, lower-priority lane. Naming must serve
that purpose rather than suggest translation, invented stories or a general
video editor. See [Product Vision](../PRODUCT_VISION.md),
[Non Goals](../NON_GOALS.md) and [User Workflow](../USER_WORKFLOW.md).

Dan confirmed both **.com and .org** are required for every alternative,
with standard registration only and a primary-domain first-year price of
at most **US$50**. He requested exploration of all three naming structures.
Acceptance is a small shortlist whose exact root domains have positive
current registrar offers, recorded prices/status/times/sources, concrete
tradeoffs and a retain-or-change recommendation. This is research only.
Dan subsequently requested two further batches of ten candidates; the dated
follow-ups below extend the choices without adopting a name or changing
production.

## Existing uses and naming conflicts

HeartWord has direct overlap with Bible software: Michael Baas's
[HeartWord](https://heartword.app/) collects and memorizes Bible passages,
and Timetells publishes
[Heartword Scripture Memory](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.timetells.heartword).
An additional
[Heartword Stoic-quotes app](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/heartword-daily-stoic-quotes/id6762086736)
uses the same spoken name outside the Bible category. These uses create
discovery and differentiation concerns; they do not establish legal rights
or legal infringement.

Exact-name searches for Word in Reach/wordinreach, ScriptureKin/Scripture
Kin, and Every Tongue Media/everytonguemedia did not surface an obvious
exact-name business in this check. Search coverage is incomplete. Word in
Reach has a relevant near-name use in
[WordReach by 201 Studio](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.studio201.wordreach),
a word game. Every Tongue Media shares common mission wording with
[Lifeword](https://lifeword.org/who-we-are/about/), which explicitly describes
heart-language media for every tongue, and with the film “Every Voice,
Every Tongue” shown by
[Impact Film Lab](https://impactfilmlab.com/).
ScriptureKin had no obvious exact-name use in the searched results, but its
Scripture component is descriptive and widely used.

These are current web and app-store checks, not a trademark search or legal
clearance. Before adopting a final public brand, check relevant business and
trademark records, confusingly similar names and social handles in the
intended markets. Do not interpret domain availability as naming rights.

## Three naming structures

These examples use only roots whose .com and .org passed above. No
availability claim is made for extra Studio, Films or Illustrated domains.

| Structure | Concrete example | Tradeoff |

| **One umbrella name — recommended** | Organization: Word in Reach. Software: Word in Reach Studio. Outputs: Word in Reach Films and Word in Reach Illustrated. | One identity and one checked domain pair; every output builds recognition for the same mission. Describe AI in the studio's positioning rather than requiring it in every viewer-facing name. |
| Organization and studio together, separate output brand | Word in Reach organization and Studio; ScriptureKin Films and ScriptureKin Illustrated as the output family. | Lets films emphasize Scripture while the studio explains technology. Two identities, two domain pairs and more credit/communication work. Both pairs would total $38.12 initially at the observed prices. |
| Separate organization and studio names | Word in Reach organization; ScriptureKin Studio as its software; Word in Reach Films and Illustrated as outputs. | Makes the software distinct if it later serves other producers. Today it introduces another name for the same single-producer workflow without a demonstrated benefit. |

The existing tagline, “Scripture media for every heart language,” remains
suitable for the recommended umbrella. The existing studio tagline can also
carry forward: “An AI production studio for Scripture in every heart
language.” No translation or free-form storytelling claim is introduced.

## Consequences of selecting a different name

A later, explicitly authorized transition would update public wordmarks and
logo, application headings/browser title, README and current brand references,
website/domain/email/links, distribution profiles and templates for future
credits. Existing illustrated and film edition labels would need an explicit
transition policy. The chapter title card continues to identify the book,
chapter and heart language; a naming change does not justify a persistent
watermark or replacing Scripture titles.

Repository/package identifiers, HEARTWORD_DATA_DIR, database filenames,
project/shot/asset IDs, agent identities, provenance paths and historical
review/edit records do not need to change for a public rebrand. Preserve
accepted media and released/held film files. Any later credit revision should
be a new reviewed version with history, not an overwrite or a regeneration
of the Scripture performance. This research authorizes no transition.
